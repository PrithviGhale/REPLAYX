# REPLAYX 0.3.1-alpha.1 — first public preview

**Capture everything. Miss nothing.**

REPLAYX is a free, open-source Instant Replay and screen recorder for Windows gamers: no account, no ads, no telemetry. This is the first public **alpha**. It works well on the machines it has been tested on, but it has not been used on many PCs yet. Please report anything that goes wrong.

## Highlights

- **Instant Replay:** keeps the last 30 seconds to 30 minutes of gameplay on disk. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>F10</kbd> and the moment is saved as a clip, usually in about a second.
- **Recording:** <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>F9</kbd>, joining Instant Replay without a gap.
- **Voice Enhancer (new):** cleaner headset audio with REPLAYX's own processing. Presets *Natural*, *Clear*, *Broadcast* and *Noise-Free*; noise suppression (RNNoise) at *Light*, *Balanced* or *Strong*; recording gain that can't clip; your own presets.
  - **Test mic** lets you hear yourself live and switch between *Enhanced* and *Original* at the same loudness.
  - **Compare** plays the original and enhanced version of the same take.
- **Separate audio tracks:** game, Discord, system sound and microphone each on their own track, plus a full mix. New devices are detected automatically.
- **Quality:** exact output sizes, 30–144 fps, H.264 / HEVC / AV1 on NVIDIA, Intel and AMD encoders, and constant-quality presets that save space. A storage estimate shows how long your drive lasts.
- **Clips library:** clips sorted into folders per game automatically (140 games recognised), browsed as *All clips* or *By game* tiles, with sorting, filters, search, rename, MP4 export and delete.
- **Games and playtime (optional):** see how long you play each game today, this week or this month, with charts and a page per game. Only on your PC; off until you turn it on.
- **Status overlay:** small icons for Instant Replay, microphone and recording over every app and game. They are visible to you and never appear in your clips.
- **Tray:** closing the window keeps REPLAYX running in the tray (click the icon to reopen), with the real state in its menu; it asks before quitting during a recording.
- **Your shortcuts:** including Alt+F10 when no other program (such as NVIDIA's overlay) uses it; REPLAYX tells you when a shortcut is taken and keeps the previous one working.
- **Polished start:** a quick animated start-up screen showing what is really loading, and a one-minute guided tour on first launch (skippable, replayable from Settings).

## Install

1. Download `REPLAYX_0.3.1-alpha.1_x64-setup.exe` and `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
2. Check the file: `Get-FileHash .\REPLAYX_0.3.1-alpha.1_x64-setup.exe`. It must match the value in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
3. Run the installer. Builds are not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may say *Windows protected your PC*. Choose **More info → Run anyway** only if the checksum matched.

Uninstalling never deletes your clips in `Videos\REPLAYX`.

## Requirements

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). A GPU with hardware video encoding is recommended (NVIDIA, Intel or AMD); AV1 needs an RTX 40, Arc/Core Ultra or RX 7000 GPU. Per-app audio tracks need Windows 10 build 20348 or later. The WebView2 runtime is installed automatically if missing.

## Known limitations

- Alpha: tested mostly on one laptop (Intel Iris Xe + NVIDIA RTX 4070 Laptop).
- The Voice Enhancer has been measured with synthetic speech; real-world listening feedback is welcome. Keyboard clicks are only partly removed.
- The overlay can't show over exclusive full-screen games (a Windows rule); use borderless or windowed mode.
- Not code-signed yet.

The full list is in [KNOWN_LIMITATIONS](../KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md); troubleshooting tips are in the [README](../../README.md#troubleshooting).
