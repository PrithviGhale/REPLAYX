**REPLAYX** is a free, open-source game recorder for Windows: Instant Replay, smooth hardware-accelerated recording, clips sorted by game, a Voice Enhancer for your microphone and local playtime tracking. No account, no ads, nothing uploaded.

This alpha fixes an audio error that could stop REPLAYX from starting capture, and makes the Clips folder button open the right folder.

## What's New

- **Audio that survives device changes.** Plugging in or unplugging a headset, a Bluetooth headset switching modes, or waking your PC from sleep no longer stops REPLAYX. If a device changes while capture is starting, REPLAYX waits a moment and tries again; if it changes while you play, REPLAYX reconnects audio by itself. Footage saved so far is kept, and a recording continues as a new part.

## Improvements and Fixes

- Fixed: **"Audio initialization failed … 0x88890004"** when turning on Instant Replay or recording while an audio device was switching. Audio errors are now explained in plain words.
- Fixed: the **folder button in Clips** opened your Documents folder instead of the REPLAYX clips folder. Your existing setting is repaired automatically.
- Playtime tracking stays on by default and can be turned off in **Settings → Playtime tracking**. It costs about 0.002% of one CPU core and never touches your game.

## Installation

1. Download `REPLAYX_0.3.1-alpha.6_x64-setup.exe`.
2. *(Recommended)* Check it in PowerShell with `Get-FileHash .\REPLAYX_0.3.1-alpha.6_x64-setup.exe` and compare with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
3. Run the installer (per-user, no administrator rights). Builds aren't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may appear: choose **More info → Run anyway** if the checksum matched.

Updating from an earlier alpha: just run the new installer. Your clips, settings and playtime are kept.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). A GPU with hardware video encoding (NVIDIA, Intel or AMD) is recommended.

## Known Limitations

- Alpha software: tested mostly on one laptop (Intel Iris Xe + NVIDIA RTX 4070 Laptop).
- Not code-signed yet, and in-app updates are not enabled yet.
- If a specific microphone you selected is unplugged for good, REPLAYX tries to reconnect three times and then stops capture with a message; choose another microphone in **Audio**.
- The status overlay can't appear over exclusive full-screen games (a Windows rule); use borderless or windowed mode.
