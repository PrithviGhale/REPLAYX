# REPLAYX v0.3.1-alpha.9

## What's new

- **Errors you can fix in one click.**
  - Problems now show a short title, one plain sentence and buttons that
    lead to the fix: *Fix audio*, *Windows sound settings*, *Retry*,
    *Change shortcuts* and others.
  - *Retry* repeats exactly what failed, such as *Retry recording* or
    *Retry Instant Replay*, and the problem stays on screen until it
    works.
  - REPLAYX opens the right page and highlights the right setting.
  - Windows error codes are still available under *Technical details*.
- **"Game detected" appears once per game.** Alt+Tab, minimising and
  switching between full screen and borderless no longer bring it back,
  however long you play. Closing and relaunching the game shows it again,
  even if you relaunch it right away.
- **A new look for notices.**
  - *Game detected*, *Recording started* and *Replay saved* show the game's
    icon, a clear title and a status line.
  - They appear on the monitor the game is on.
  - They animate gently, and only fade when Windows animations are turned
    off.
- **Lighter in the background.** While REPLAYX waits in the tray, it uses
  about half the memory (about 180 MB instead of 370 MB) and about 40% less
  CPU than before.
- **Windows Graphics Capture (experimental) is smoother and lighter.**
  - It captures once per output frame and only raises the rate when a game
    would otherwise get repeated frames.
  - It scales frames on the GPU before encoding.
  - The yellow capture border is gone.
  - Turn it on in *Video → Capture method*. The default is unchanged.

## Fixes

- When a sound device is switching (for example a Bluetooth headset or a
  virtual sound device), REPLAYX now waits up to 10 seconds for it to settle
  before reporting "0x88890004" (before: about 4 seconds). This was tested
  with simulated device errors, not yet with physical headsets.
- REPLAYX opens with its default settings, instead of not opening at all,
  if its settings file can't be read.
  - A damaged settings file is kept as a copy next to it, and you're told
    its name.
  - If another program is using the file, REPLAYX doesn't change it.
    Restart REPLAYX to load your settings.
- A microphone that stops sending sound is no longer reported as "access
  blocked".
- The Performance page's metric cards are laid out correctly again.
- An empty Clips library now offers to turn on Instant Replay.

## Known limitations

- On laptops whose screen is driven by the integrated GPU, recording can
  still lower the FPS of CPU-heavy games noticeably. Measured in Fortnite:
  about 19% with Windows Graphics Capture and Quick Sync. Screens driven by
  the dedicated GPU are much less affected.
- Windows Graphics Capture remains experimental; Desktop Duplication stays
  the default.
- Builds are not code-signed yet. Check the installer against
  `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

Updating keeps your settings, clips and playtime.
