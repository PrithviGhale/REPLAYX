# Code of Conduct

REPLAYX follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want taking part in REPLAYX to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent; most bug reports come from someone whose recording just failed.
- Give and accept feedback on code and ideas, not on people.
- Keep reports useful: steps, versions, hardware. Remove other people's voices, chats and account details from anything you attach.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, slurs or sexualised language and imagery.
- Publishing other people's private information, including recordings of them, without permission.
- Trolling, personal or political attacks, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who do so.

Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub, either by contacting [@PrithviGhale](https://github.com/PrithviGhale) or through the repository's private security reporting. Reports are handled confidentially.
